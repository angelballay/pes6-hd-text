PES6 HD Text 1.0.1 - fonts
==========================

Each .bin file is the game's text font (font 53) redrawn at 4x from a free font.
Letters the free font does not have (Japanese, symbols) are the game's own letters, upscaled.
The license of each source font is in the "licenses" folder.
